What it does

  • One ring per service in the menu bar, plus the limit that's closest to running out.
  • Claude's session and weekly limits, ChatGPT's Codex 5-hour and weekly windows, and Cursor's weekly or monthly allowance.
  • A global shortcut (⌥⌘U) for a quick peek from any app.
  • Alerts at 80% and 95%, launch at login, light and dark mode.

Install

  1. Download the zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Move Headroom.app to Applications and open it.
  3. If macOS can't verify the developer, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway (once).
  4. Click the rings, then Settings… › Services to sign in. Use email sign-in; passkeys don't work there.

Private by design

Your sign-ins stay on your Mac. Headroom only talks to claude.ai, chatgpt.com and cursor.com, the same way their own usage pages do. There are no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and every line of code is public.

Headroom is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI or Anysphere (Cursor). These services don't offer official usage APIs, so a change on their side can break a service until Headroom is updated. MIT License.
